What a whole-home repipe involves

A repipe means removing your home's existing hot and cold water supply lines and replacing them with new piping throughout - typically PEX or copper, depending on your preference and the layout of the home. It does not mean tearing your house apart. On a typical single-story Merced home, we run new lines through attic space, wall cavities, and under cabinets, making small, strategic access cuts in drywall rather than gutting rooms.

The scope covers every hot and cold line from the main shutoff to each fixture: kitchen sink, bathroom sinks, tubs, showers, toilets, laundry hookups, exterior hose bibs, and the water heater connections. We pressure-test the new system, tie it into your existing shutoffs and fixtures, and patch the access openings. Most homes we repipe were originally plumbed in galvanized steel (common in older Merced neighborhoods), polybutylene, or early copper that's now pitting from Central Valley water chemistry. When we're done, you have a modern, code-compliant supply system that should outlast the rest of the house.

Our whole-home repipe process

  1. Free in-home assessment. We walk your Merced home, inspect visible piping, check pressure and water quality, and identify how many fixtures and runs are involved.
  2. Written estimate and material choice. You get a firm quote with PEX and copper options laid out side by side, plus a realistic project timeline.
  3. Permits pulled. We handle the City of Merced or Merced County permitting so the work is inspected and documented.
  4. Prep and protection. Floors covered, furniture moved as needed, water shut off, and access openings planned to minimize drywall cuts.
  5. New pipe installation. Fresh supply lines run to every fixture, secured, insulated where appropriate, and tied into shutoffs.
  6. Pressure test and inspection. We pressure-test the system, then coordinate the municipal inspection.
  7. Patch, clean, and walkthrough. Drywall patched to ready-for-paint, site cleaned, and a final walkthrough so you know exactly what was done.

What a whole-home repipe typically costs

Repipe pricing varies more than most plumbing jobs because no two houses are the same. The biggest cost drivers are square footage, number of bathrooms and fixtures, number of stories, slab vs. raised foundation, whether you choose PEX or copper, and how much drywall access we need to open. A compact single-bath home in central Merced is a very different job from a four-bath two-story out toward Franklin or Beachwood.

Estimates typically include all new supply piping, fittings, shutoff valves, permit fees, pressure testing, and drywall patching to a paint-ready finish. Painting, tile repair, and any fixture upgrades you choose to add are usually quoted separately so you can decide what's worth doing while the walls are open. We give you a written, itemized estimate for free - call us and we'll come take a look.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a whole-home repipe take?
Most Merced homes are repiped in two to five working days depending on size and layout. Drywall patching adds another day or two.

Will I be without water the whole time?
No. We shut water off only during active tie-ins - usually a few hours at a time - and restore service at the end of each workday so you can shower and use toilets overnight.

PEX or copper - which is better for my home?
Both are excellent. PEX is flexible, freeze-tolerant, faster to install, and generally less expensive. Copper is rigid, long-proven, and preferred by some homeowners for exposed runs. We'll recommend based on your home's layout and your budget.

Do I need a repipe or just a repair?
If you've had two or more pinhole leaks in the past year, discolored water, or your home still has galvanized or polybutylene supply lines, a repipe is usually the smarter long-term move. We'll tell you honestly if a targeted repair will get you more years.

Do you pull permits?
Yes. Every repipe we do in Merced is permitted and inspected. That protects your home value and your insurance coverage.

Do you repipe rental properties for landlords and property managers?
Absolutely. We work with Central Valley property managers regularly and can coordinate access, tenant notifications, and scheduling around occupancy.
