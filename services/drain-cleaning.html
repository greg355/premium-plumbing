Signs You Need Professional Drain Cleaning

  • Water drains slower than it used to — or not at all
  • Gurgling sounds from drains or the toilet when water runs elsewhere
  • Sewage odors coming from sinks, tubs or floor drains
  • More than one fixture backing up at the same time (a main-line warning sign)
  • Clogs that keep coming back after store-bought drain cleaner
Multiple drains backing up at once?

That usually means a main sewer line blockage — don't wait. Call (209) 669-2090 and we'll prioritize getting out to you, often the same day.

How We Clear Your Drain

We don't just punch a hole through the clog and drive away. Our process is built to fix the problem and prove it's fixed:

  • Diagnose first. We identify where the blockage is and what's causing it — grease, hair, scale, roots or a damaged pipe.
  • Clear it with the right tool. Professional augers and snakes for typical clogs; hydro jetting when the line needs a true wall-to-wall cleaning.
  • Verify with a camera. On main lines we run a sewer camera so you can see the line is clear — and catch any pipe damage before it becomes an emergency.
  • Leave it clean. Shoe covers, drop cloths, and no mess left behind. It's your home, and we treat it that way.

Chemical Drain Cleaners vs. Professional Cleaning

Store-bought chemical cleaners can eat through more than the clog — repeated use damages older pipes, and they rarely remove the buildup that caused the clog in the first place. That's why the same drain keeps clogging every few months. A professional cleaning removes the blockage and the buildup, so the fix lasts.
