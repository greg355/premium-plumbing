What drain cleaning involves

Drain cleaning is more than pouring chemicals down a pipe and hoping. When we arrive, we identify where the clog actually is - a single fixture branch, a shared vent, or the main sewer lateral heading out to the city connection. That matters because the tool and the access point change based on the diagnosis.

For kitchen sinks packed with grease, bathroom drains full of hair and soap scum, or laundry lines coated in lint, we typically run a motorized cable (drain snake) sized to the pipe. Cables handle most household clogs cleanly. For heavier buildup, roots in older Ceres neighborhoods, or repeat backups, we recommend hydro jetting - a high-pressure water system that scours the pipe walls back to bare pipe rather than just poking a hole through the blockage. When the cause isn't obvious, we run a camera down the line to see exactly what's happening: bellied pipe, offset joint, root intrusion, or foreign object. You see the footage on the screen with us. No guessing, no upsell theater.

Our drain cleaning process

  1. Call and dispatch. Tell us what's happening - which fixture, how long, whether water is coming up somewhere it shouldn't. We schedule you promptly.
  2. On-site diagnosis. The plumber walks the property, checks affected fixtures, and locates the correct cleanout or access point.
  3. Written estimate. You get a clear price for the work before we start. No surprise add-ons.
  4. Clear the line. We use the right tool for the job - cable for standard clogs, hydro jetting for grease, sludge, or roots.
  5. Camera inspection (when warranted). If the line has backed up before or the clog looks unusual, we run a camera to confirm the pipe is clear and identify any underlying issue.
  6. Test and clean up. We run water through the system, confirm proper flow, wipe down the work area, and haul off any debris.
  7. Follow-up. We tell you honestly whether this was a one-time clog or a symptom of something bigger, and what (if anything) to watch for.

What drain cleaning typically costs

Drain cleaning in Ceres is priced based on how the line is accessed, what tool is required, and how much time the job takes. A simple bathroom sink or tub clog cleared through an existing cleanout is at the low end. A main sewer line cleared from an exterior cleanout runs higher. Hydro jetting costs more than cabling because the equipment and setup are more involved, but it often saves money over the long run by fully clearing the pipe. Camera inspections may be quoted separately or bundled in.

Costs go up when there's no accessible cleanout and we need to pull a toilet or open a wall, when tree roots have invaded the line, or when the job requires overtime hours. Frequently asked questions

How fast can you get to my house in Ceres?
Ceres is a short run up Highway 99 from our Turlock shop. We work to get on-site as quickly as possible and can often respond promptly for urgent backups.

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning after hours?
Sewage backups and overflowing drains don't wait for business hours. Call the main line and we'll work to get a plumber dispatched.

Should I just use a store-bought drain cleaner first?
We don't recommend it. Caustic drain chemicals can damage older pipes, harm fixtures, and make the plumber's job (and your bill) worse if they don't clear the clog. A proper cabling or jetting is faster and safer.

What's the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?
A cable (snake) punches through a clog to restore flow. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe. For grease, sludge, or roots, jetting is the better long-term solution.

My drain keeps clogging every few months. Is that normal?
No. Repeat backups usually mean roots, a bellied section of pipe, or a partial collapse. A camera inspection will show exactly what's happening so you're not paying for the same cabling four times a year.

Do you handle rental properties and commercial buildings?
Yes. We work with landlords, property managers, and commercial facility managers throughout the Central Valley on both scheduled maintenance and urgent calls.
